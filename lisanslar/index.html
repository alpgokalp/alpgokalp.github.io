Every third-party asset used in Traffic Cutter is listed here with its source, licence and the changes made to it. The same list is shown inside the game under Settings > More > Sources · Privacy. All vehicles in the game are fictional; no real brand names, logos or trademarks appear in the app.

Player cars Voltra R — "Car Concept" by Eric Chadwick, model and textures © 2024 Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH. Source, CC BY 4.0. Modified: logo texture, licence plate, emblem, wipers and original wheels removed; own lights, thruster nozzles, procedural wheels and paint/body-kit options added; geometry simplified and textures recompressed for mobile.

Vandal V12 — "Fictional supercar - V12 Goblin" by Olli Teittinen, source, CC BY 4.0. glTF conversion by gecko0307 (ChillwaveDrive). Modified: renamed, original "Goblin" nose emblem removed, materials merged, procedural wheels, geometry simplified.

Nebula 050 — "Concept Car 050" by Unity Fan, source, CC0 1.0. glTF conversion by gecko0307. Modified: renamed, materials merged, procedural wheels, geometry simplified.

Proto S — original procedural model made for the game.

Traffic Sedan and crossover SUV — Innerscene free 3D parts library (sedan, SUV), listed as CC0. Modified: normalised, batched, repainted, simplified.

Delivery truck — "Cesium Milk Truck" © 2017 Cesium, source, CC BY 4.0. Modified: normalised, paint and scale variants, batched. The Cesium logo on the model is kept as part of the licensed asset; no endorsement by Cesium is implied.

Tram and metrobus — original procedural models; stylised city transit, not replicas of any operator's vehicles.

Ramp truck, birds, helicopters, light plane, private jet, airliner, airship and balloons — original models made for the game (built in code and exported with three.js); fictional liveries, no real operator logo.

Maps — the city roads and the Tour of Türkiye roads are drawn in code for the game (no map-service data or photos).

Software and fonts Three.js r160 with GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils — MIT licence (see vendor-THREE-LICENSE.txt). Barlow Condensed, Overpass and Overpass Mono — SIL Open Font License 1.1.

All vehicles in the game are fictional. Traffic Cutter is not affiliated with or endorsed by any car manufacturer.

Tools / libraries - meshoptimizer (meshopt decoder, EXT_meshopt_compression) by Arseny Kapoulkine — MIT, see meshoptimizer.txt. Models in veri1-4.js are compressed with it at build time.
